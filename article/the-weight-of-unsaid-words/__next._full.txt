11:Td21,I like to document life. Torn pages from a notebook, letters from my high school friends, old movie tickets, a beat-up friendship bracelet my friend made for me in high school. I have a folder full of things like this that I have never been able to throw away. None of it is worth anything, but I hold on to it all as tokens of a life lived. The same instinct is why I write. I am a compulsive journaller. I carry a couple of notebooks with me all the time. Constantly jotting down streams of thought in the form of voice notes or messages to myself on WhatsApp. What I struggle with is putting my writing out in the world. See, at least to me, writing is the most naked form of creation. When I write and share publicly it makes me a lot more vulnerable. Words have immense power and the way you string a combination of words together can expose a lot about the person writing them. I try my best at it but I just can't pretend to be smart. Like anyone I am trying to find my place in the world so I write not to preach but to document myself, and that leaves me more exposed than I want to be. Weirdly, the other way I express what I am feeling is through design. I make an obscene amount of posters to say what I am feeling, but it never bothers me to put that out. I am as emotional about my design as I am about my writing, and yet I have no problem publishing my designs. It took me a while to see it. Design doesn't bother me because I can hide my feelings behind aesthetics. Nobody else knows what I am trying to say except me, which makes me feel safe. But unlike design, writing has no mask. There is no visual to stand behind. It's just me and my thoughts. Raw, naked and exposed. And under all of it, the fear I have been avoiding writing down: I feel people will see through me and realise I am dumb. There, I said it. Honestly, this is a problem I have created for myself. The feedback from Timecapsule has always been really nice and I have made so many amazing connections because of how much people resonated with what I say. The audience I am afraid of has actually never turned up. Timecapsule was always meant for me rather than an audience, and I have made that audience so much more important in my head than it needs to be. I don't think I can switch off the voice in my head but what I will be aiming for is turning it down. Writing, for me, is documentation. The same way a photograph pauses a memory in time, my writing pauses an emotion. And I am messy. My emotions are messy and hence my writing will be too. It will always be hard for me to put myself out there and one essay does not fix that. But I plan to keep pushing regardless. I have received so many good things because of Timecapsule. The people who reached out to me, the friends I have made through this and the opportunities I have gotten, all came from something I was too anxious to release. So this is what Timecapsule is going to be from here on out. Not a portfolio of finished and polished essays but a folder of messy streams of thought. Whatever I am actually thinking about that week, half-formed and unresolved, notes on things I am reading, arguments I will disagree with in six months. I cant promise that what i write will be the most valuable piece you read in the day. But i promise you this, it will be the most honest.
